\section*{Learning points / Take-home messages}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Record-level separation provides a stricter estimate of ECG classifier generalisation than random beat-level allocation.
  \item The CNN--Conformer ranked first on aggregate metrics, but SVEB and Fusion recognition remained near zero.
  \item Weighted-F1 alone can hide severe minority-class failures; class-specific metrics and confusion patterns are essential.
  \item Longer rhythm context, the second ECG channel, and development-set minority-class strategies should be tested before external evaluation.
\end{itemize}
