Figure 1. Held-out confusion matrix for the proposed CNN--Conformer. The most consequential errors are concentrated in the minority rhythms: SVEB is predominantly assigned to Normal or VEB, while Fusion is predominantly assigned to VEB. Absolute class support should therefore be considered alongside the diagonal counts.

Figure 2. One-vs-rest receiver operating characteristic curves on the fixed held-out cohort. Normal and VEB show the strongest ranking discrimination, whereas Fusion is weak. AUC should be interpreted with the corresponding precision--recall behaviour because the class distribution is highly imbalanced.

Figure 3. Precision--recall curves on the fixed held-out cohort. Minority-class precision declines rapidly as recall is increased, making the rare-rhythm limitation more apparent than in aggregate accuracy alone.
