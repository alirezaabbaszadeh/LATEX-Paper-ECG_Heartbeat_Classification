\section{Conclusion}
This work presented a two-stage waveform analysis pipeline that converts three-beat Morlet scalograms into arrhythmia predictions using a lightweight Conformer, yielding 0.60 accuracy and 0.26 macro-F1 on the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database. These results surpass the CNN-only, CNN-LSTM, and attention-only baselines packaged with the repository, demonstrating the value of hybrid inductive biases even when minority classes remain difficult.\cite{Hsieh2025Economic,Hempel2025ECGAging,Gliner2025Interpretability}

The reproducibility-first design—including shared preprocessing scripts, training code, raw predictions, figure notebooks, and released weights—aligns with open-access best practices and extends BioMedical Engineering OnLine efforts to couple interpretable modelling with resilient sensing pipelines across physiological domains.\cite{Jiang2025Hybrid,Stern2024InBed,Aslan2025Poincare} Future work will integrate uncertainty-aware calibration, targeted augmentation for supraventricular and fusion rhythms, and prospective telemonitoring studies so that validations extend beyond public benchmarks and closer to routine cardiovascular care.
