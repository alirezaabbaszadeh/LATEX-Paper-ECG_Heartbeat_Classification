\section{Conclusion}
We presented a reproducible ECG heartbeat classification pipeline that combines Morlet wavelet scalograms with a lightweight Conformer encoder. Leveraging the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, the approach delivered substantial improvements over attention-only, CNN-LSTM, and feature-engineered baselines while providing comprehensive diagnostics for downstream clinical assessment. Performance on rare fusion and supraventricular beats remains limited and macro-F1 reaches only 0.26 on the held-out test cohort. Even so, the reported gains and open-source tooling offer a practical foundation for telemonitoring decision support where automated alerts are paired with cardiologist oversight. Future extensions that expand datasets, enrich contextual modelling beyond three-beat windows, evaluate calibration behaviour, and incorporate clinician feedback loops are expected to further advance robust, deployable arrhythmia screening in line with Journal of Electrocardiology priorities.
