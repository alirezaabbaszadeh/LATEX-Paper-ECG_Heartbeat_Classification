Journal of Electrocardiology (Elsevier)
Scope

Focuses on clinical and experimental studies of the heart’s electrical activity, including electrocardiography, vectorcardiography, arrhythmias, pacing, instrumentation, and related computational applications.

Guide for Authors

Core article types and limits: Original Research (≤5,000 words, ≤40 references), Reviews (10–15 double-spaced pages including figures/tables, ≤3 illustrations, ≤40 references), Technical articles (10–15 double-spaced pages, ≤25 references), Case Reports (3–5 pages, ≤8 references, 100-word abstract), and other short formats with explicit word/figure caps.

Required structure guidance: manuscripts must be divided into clearly defined sections with headings; acknowledgements precede references; footnotes numbered consecutively.

Formatting essentials: provide editable source files (Word single column or LaTeX), include a structured or unstructured abstract ≤250 words, supply 1–7 keywords, and submit 3–5 bullet-point highlights plus (optional) graphical abstract meeting minimum dimensions.

Citation style: numerical in-text citations in square brackets ordered by appearance, abbreviated journal names per LTWA, shortened page ranges, and dataset/software citation formats.

Figures/tables: submit images separately, supply captions, meet minimum dpi (e.g., ≥300 dpi for halftones, ≥1000 dpi for line art), and limit Review articles to three illustrations; tables must be editable text.

Data sharing: encouraged to deposit data in repositories, cite datasets, and include a data availability statement (Elsevier “Option B”).

BioMedical Engineering OnLine (BMC)
Scope

Publishes open-access research across biomedical engineering, covering topics from bioinformatics and signal processing to devices, imaging, rehabilitation, and cardiovascular systems; excludes submissions using only simulations or solely public datasets.

Guide for Authors

General manuscript preparation: double-line spacing, page/line numbers, SI units, no manual page breaks; acceptable formats include DOC/DOCX, RTF, or LaTeX with editable sources required.

Figures: numbered in order, composite panels combined, titles ≤15 words, legends ≤300 words, individual files ≤10 MB, and must meet standard web/PDF dimensions (600/1200 px web; 85 mm or 170 mm width in PDF at ~300 dpi).

References and supporting files: cite only published or in-press works; web links assigned reference numbers; tables formatted via word processor table tools; additional files allowed (≤20 MB) with explicit metadata and repository preference for datasets.

Data & materials: strongly encourage depositing datasets in repositories and detailing availability statements; follow FORCE11 principles and include repository identifiers/DOIs.

Editorial policies: extensive requirements for ethics, consent, data availability, AI use, reporting standards, and competing interests apply to all submissions.

Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (Elsevier)
Scope

Official journal of EAMBES that emphasizes cross-disciplinary work on the measurement and analysis of biomedical signals and images for clinical diagnosis, monitoring, and patient management. Article categories span reviews (10,000 words), full papers (~5,000 words), short communications (~2,000 words), technical notes (5,000 words with ≤5 illustrations), and perspectives.

Guide for Authors

Formatting: submit editable Word (single-column) or LaTeX source files; provide a ≤250-word abstract, 1–7 keywords, and section numbering (1, 1.1, etc.) with acknowledgements before references. Highlights are mandatory (3–5 bullets, ≤85 characters) and graphical abstracts are encouraged (≥531×1328 px, TIFF/EPS/PDF/Office formats). Figures must be uploaded separately with logical names, captions, and minimum resolution (≥300 dpi halftones, ≥1000 dpi line art, ≥500 dpi combo art); tables remain editable text without vertical rules. Math should stay in editable form, and CRediT author contribution statements plus data availability statements are required.

Citation/data policies: use numeric references in square brackets ordered by appearance with LTWA abbreviations and DOIs where possible. Elsevier Research Data Policy Option B applies—deposit data in repositories, cite datasets/software, link identifiers, and include a data statement. Authors must disclose funding, generative AI use, competing interests, ethics approvals, and comply with inclusive language and SGBA guidance.

Computers in Biology and Medicine (Elsevier)
Scope

Focuses on computational advances applied to bioscience and medicine, covering biomedical system analysis/synthesis, medical imaging, bioinformatics, drug design, biomedical/biological signal processing, and AI foundations in healthcare. Rejects manuscripts limited to basic in silico experiments or minor model tweaks without rigorous validation.

Guide for Authors

Article types include full-length articles, reviews, letters, discussions, and tutorials. Submissions must be editable Word (single column) or LaTeX (double-column allowed) with ≤250-word abstracts, 1–7 keywords, mandatory highlights (3–5 bullets, ≤85 characters), and encouraged graphical abstracts (≥531×1328 px). First submissions need not mimic final layout, but section headings, acknowledgements before references, appendices labeling, glossary (if needed), and full CRediT author statements are expected. Figures/tables follow standard Elsevier specs (separate files, ≥300 dpi halftones, ≥1000 dpi line art, ≥500 dpi combo art, captions provided, tables as editable text without vertical rules) and videos/supplementary files must include descriptive captions. The journal endorses EQUATOR/CONSORT/STARD/PRISMA/MOOSE/CARE reporting guidelines as appropriate.

Data/citation policies: Research Data Policy Option B applies (deposit, cite/link datasets, explain restrictions). Manuscripts require data availability statements, ethics/consent approvals, clinical trial registry disclosures, funding statements, competing interests, and inclusive language plus SGBA adherence. References follow numeric square-bracket style with LTWA abbreviations and DOIs; submission checklist enforces uploading of keywords, tables, figure captions, and supplementary files.
